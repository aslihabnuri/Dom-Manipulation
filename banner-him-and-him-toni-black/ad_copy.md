# Made For Him and Him: ad copy (1:1 and 2:3 banners)

Written to the Toni Black brand guideline, Tone of Voice: calm confidence, concise, no hyperbole or slang. Caption style: line breaks instead of full stops, two emoji (👨‍👦 for the pair, 🖤 in the brand's black). The whole offer sits in the first line, inside the 125 characters shown before "See more".
