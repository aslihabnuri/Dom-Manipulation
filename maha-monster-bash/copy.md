# MAHA Monster Bash: primary text, headlines, welcome message, pre-filled message

Written for the 48 second vertical video in the Drive folder, in the voice
@monsterpadelbali already uses: English, short lines, one or two emoji, no
adjective stacking. No em dashes anywhere.

Format: 2160 x 3840, 9:16, 48.7 seconds, 60fps. Vertical, so Reels, Stories and
the Instagram feed placement all take it without a recrop. For the square feed
placement Meta will centre crop it, which cuts the end card, so either supply a
1:1 export or let it run vertical only.

**31 October 2026 is a Saturday**, and it is 63 days out. That clears the
14 day minimum flight the April report recommends for event campaigns, with a
lot of room to spare. The Padel Clinic campaign burned IDR 4.96M on a 3 day
window and never left the learning phase. Do not repeat that here.

**The video carries the voice already.** `1 COURT. 1 DAY. 1 MASSIVE PADEL
BATTLE` is the best line in the asset, and `COME IF YOU DARE` is the tone. The
written copy leans on both rather than inventing a new register.

### What the video does not say

None of this appears anywhere in the 48 seconds, so none of it appears in the
copy below. Fill it in before the campaign goes live.

- How to enter, and the entry fee
- Team format and categories, mixed or open, and whether there is a level cap
- What time the day starts and finishes
- Which WhatsApp number takes entries
- Whether spectators are welcome, and whether that costs anything

The entry route is the important one. A Click to WhatsApp campaign with no
stated way to enter converts into questions rather than registrations, and your
admin absorbs the difference.

## What the April report says about this exact format

From `Monster_Padel_Digital_Report.xlsx` in the same folder, covering
1 April to 11 May 2026, IDR 19.3M spend and 1,100 conversations.

**Brand collaborations are the best format this account has run.** Padel x
Puresia produced conversations at IDR 6,362 each against an account average of
IDR 17,572. The report's own recommendation is to replicate it with other
partners. MAHA Monster Bash is that format with two partners instead of one, so
it starts from the strongest position in the account's history.

**Sports Enthusiast is both the cheapest segment and the right one here.**
It carried 49.1 percent of all conversations at IDR 12,017 each. For a
competitive tournament that audience is also the actual buyer, which is not
true of every campaign this account runs. Youth Academy, the kids programme,
paid IDR 28,184 per conversation precisely because the parent it needed to
reach sits in the expensive 35 to 44 bracket. This event has no such tension.

**Suggested targeting**, following the report: Sports Enthusiast first, 18 to
34, both genders, with female weighted higher since female converted at
IDR 14,411 against IDR 14,879 for male. Layer a retargeting set over everyone
who engaged with Monster, MAHA or Padelicious in the last 180 days, and put
Angle 2 and Angle 5 there.

The prize pool is the strongest fact in this campaign, so most of these put a
number or a challenge inside that window. Hook lengths in brackets.

### How to run the test

Angles 1, 3 and 4 to cold Sports Enthusiast. Angles 2 and 5 to retargeting,
since a dare and a request for a partner both land harder on someone who
already knows the club. Same video, same headline, primary text as the only
variable. Minimum 14 day flight, and the report would say 21 for something this
size. You have 63 days, so there is no reason to run it short.

carries the number. If the primary text opened on the number, the headline

## 3. Welcome message

One paragraph, no line breaks. No address, no opening hours, no service list.
Its only jobs are to confirm the person reached the right place and to ask for
the one thing that lets you move them forward, which for a doubles tournament
is whether they already have a partner.

```
Hi, and thanks for messaging Monster Social Club Bali 🎾 You have come to the right place for MAHA Monster Bash, our Halloween padel battle on Saturday 31 October with a Rp 300 juta prize pool. Tell us your name and whether you already have a partner, and one of our team will send you the format and how to enter 💚
```

### Shorter

```
Hi, welcome to Monster Social Club Bali 🎾 Good to have you here. For MAHA Monster Bash on 31 October, send us your name and whether you have a partner yet, and we will come straight back with the format and how to enter 💚
```
