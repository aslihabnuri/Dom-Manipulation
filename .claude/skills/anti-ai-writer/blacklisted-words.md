# Kata, Frasa, dan Kalimat Pembuka yang Dilarang

Ini adalah penanda tulisan AI yang sudah teridentifikasi secara statistik lewat beberapa riset (Carnegie Mellon 2025, Wikipedia Signs of AI Writing, analisis 52 juta postingan Buffer). Jangan pernah pakai kata-kata ini. Ganti dengan alternatif yang lebih konkret atau susun ulang kalimatnya.

## Ciri Khas Kata Pembuka per Model AI

Hindari mengawali jawaban dengan kata-kata berikut, karena tercatat sebagai kecenderungan khas tiap model:

- **ChatGPT** cenderung mulai dengan: "sebagai," "ya," "tentu," "berikut," "dalam," "untuk," "membuat," "pasti," "judul," "yang"
- **Claude** cenderung mulai dengan: "dalam," "dari," "ini," "bagaimana," "ya," "judul," "menurut," "yang," "berdasarkan," "berikut"
- **Grok** cenderung mulai dengan: "langkah," "pendahuluan," "ya," "membuat," "untuk," "judul," "dalam," "pasti"
- **Gemini** cenderung mulai dengan: "saya," "membuat," "sementara," "berikut," "ya," "ini," "yang"
- **DeepSeek** cenderung mulai dengan: "berdasarkan," "ya," "langkah," "komprehensif," "berikut," "untuk," "membuat," "judul," "pasti"

## Kosakata Khas AI per Periode (sebagai konteks)

- **2023–pertengahan 2024 (era GPT-4):** tambahan, memiliki (boasts), diperkuat, krusial, mendalami, menekankan, abadi, meraih, rumit, interaksi, kunci, lanskap, teliti, penting, menegaskan, tapestri, bukti nyata, berharga, dinamis
- **Pertengahan 2024–pertengahan 2025 (era GPT-4o):** selaras dengan, diperkuat, krusial, menekankan, meningkatkan, abadi, memupuk, menyoroti, penting, menampilkan, menegaskan, dinamis
- **Pertengahan 2025 dan seterusnya (era GPT-5):** menekankan, meningkatkan, menyoroti, menampilkan
